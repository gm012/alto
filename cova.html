ALTO PRODUCT / 002 Coming soon

Expense tracking
made clear.

COVA is a mobile expense capture and tracking app in development at ALTO Studio. We’re bringing receipts, people and business spending into one structured workflow.

Talk to us about COVA ↗Explore the idea ↓

Concept preview. COVA is not yet available to download. No launch date has been announced.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Built to close
the expense gap.

Travel spend often becomes messy: slips get lost, costs reach the wrong company or person, and reimbursements slow down. We’re creating COVA to give every expense a clear record, an owner and a place in the business.

Proof gets lost.

Paper receipts, scattered photos and incomplete records leave teams chasing the details.

Costs get mixed up.

People working across trips, projects and companies need a reliable way to assign each expense.

Visibility comes too late.

Finance and managers need clearer records to review spending and keep reimbursements moving.

02 / THE PLANNED WORKFLOW

Capture. Categorise.
Assign. Review.

A practical flow for daily use, from a receipt in your hand to a record your team can work with.

01 / CAPTURE

Keep the receipt.

Take a photo or upload a slip, keeping proof attached to the expense.

02 / CATEGORISE

Give spend structure.

Organise costs into clear categories such as travel, meals or accommodation.

03 / ASSIGN

Put it in the right place.

Link each expense to the right person, trip, company or project.

04 / REVIEW

Move it forward.

Help managers review records and finance prepare reports and reimbursements.

Illustrative interface concepts, not live product screenshots. Example names and amounts are demonstration data.

03 / WHO WE’RE BUILDING FOR

Same journeys.
Brighter business.

Travelling teams

Capture costs while they happen, wherever work takes you.

Multiple businesses

Keep companies, departments and projects clearly separated while working in one place.

Finance and operations

Spend less time chasing slips and more time working with organised, attributable records.

04 / WHAT WE’RE WORKING TOWARDS

Less admin.
More clarity.

The first focus is a dependable expense workflow. We intend to learn from internal use and selected teams before a wider release.

Core product direction

  • Receipt capture and categorisation
  • Assignment to people, trips and businesses
  • Review and reimbursement workflows
  • Reporting and export

Further possibilities

  • Accounting-system integrations
  • Assisted data capture and automation
  • Multi-currency support
  • Additional approvals and policy controls

These are planned capabilities and areas of exploration, not a commitment to a release date or final feature set.
