01 THE PRODUCT

Work moves fast. Information should keep up.

Coordinate is designed around a simple idea: teams work better when information, activity and progress are easier to see. Instead of scattered conversations and disconnected updates, Coordinate creates one place from which a workplace can stay connected and informed.

02 BUILT FOR CLARITY

001 / WORKSPACES

One connected workspace.

002 / VISIBILITY

See what is moving.

003 / TEAMS

Keep people aligned.

Bring teams into the same digital environment so responsibilities and activity can remain connected.
