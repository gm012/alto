01 PROJECT INTENT

Make workplace coordination feel simple.

Coordinate was created around a straightforward problem: teams need a clear way to see what is available, plan ahead and book shared spaces without friction. The product brings availability, room selection, booking status and personal schedules into one connected experience.

DISCIPLINE Digital Product

01 — PRIMARY EXPERIENCE

A focused digital product system built around visibility, availability and fast workplace decision-making.

Plan. Book. Confirm.

Coordinate reduces the booking journey to the information users actually need: location, room, date, availability and confirmation.

A consistent interface language allows Coordinate to move naturally between mobile, tablet and larger digital environments.

03 THE CHALLENGE

Shared spaces create fragmented information.

Room availability, schedules and booking status are often split across tools or communicated manually. The challenge was to create one experience that gives users enough information to act quickly without turning coordination into another administrative task.

04 THE SOLUTION

One connected booking experience.

Coordinate combines location selection, room availability, calendar planning, booking confirmation and personal schedules into a focused interface. Strong hierarchy and consistent states keep the product understandable at every step.
